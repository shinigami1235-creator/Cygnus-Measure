Which ruler is this using?

Face measurements come first. Measure one distance on the patient with calipers at the first visit. The program finds those two points by itself in every photo after that.

Ink dots are the alternative. A 1 cm pair is short. Being 1 or 2 pixels off at each end already puts you 3 to 4 percent out. Draw the pair 3 to 5 cm long instead and use the Custom pair chip.

What the numbers mean

Area: how much of the region is covered by line.

Length: total line, end to end, and again per square centimeter of region.

N: how many separate lines were long enough to count.

Width: apparent width. Optical blur widens it, so read it as change and not as a true width.

Contrast: how much darker the lines are than the skin next to them.

Depth is not measured. A photo shows how dark a line is. Depth needs a replica or a 3D scan.
