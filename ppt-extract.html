Pull the photos out of a slide deck

Opens a .pptx on this machine and gives you the pictures inside it, renamed, with the camera metadata taken off. Nothing is uploaded. There is no server behind this page: close it and it is gone.

Drop a .pptx here
or click to pick one. A .zip works too.
A cropped picture is still whole in the file. PowerPoint hides the rest of the frame without removing it. Pulling the file out gives you the whole original, which can show a great deal more of the patient than the slide did. Where a slide cropped a picture, this page says so and offers to apply that crop.
Some files hold more than one photograph. A before and an after pasted side by side is one file with two faces in it, and measured as one photograph it is nonsense. Where a gutter runs the whole way down a frame this page proposes the cut and shows it. Check every one by eye before exporting. Where there is no gutter it says so and leaves it alone, because a wrong cut through a face is worse than no cut.
Slide text and speaker notes carry names and dates. They are shown further down so you can read what is in the deck. None of it goes into the pictures you export.

Where to cut


Drag a line to move it. Double click a line to remove it. Nothing is cut until you press Use this cut. Check every line against the photograph first.

Text and notes in the deck

Read this before you share anything from the deck. Anything that looks like a name, a date or a case number is highlighted by a rough match, so read the whole thing rather than trusting the highlights.

What this page does not do

It does not upload anything, and it cannot: there is nothing to upload to. It does not remove a face, blur an eye, or make a photograph anonymous, since a photograph of a face is identifiable however it is named. Consent, approval and where the files are kept afterwards are still yours to sort out.
